# Example: Monolith to Microservices Assessment

## Warning
Do not split a monolith until business boundaries, data ownership and operational maturity are clear.

## Better First Step
Move from big ball of mud to modular monolith before distributed services.

## Evidence Required Before Splitting
- Stable domain boundaries.
- Independent data ownership.
- CI/CD maturity.
- Observability.
- Team ownership model.
