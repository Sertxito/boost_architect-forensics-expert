# Pattern Catalog: Architecture Smells

## Business logic duplicated across frontend and backend

**How to detect:** look for direct evidence in code, config, runtime, logs, pipelines or dependencies.

**Why it matters:** connect the smell to business, technical and operational impact.

**Typical remediation:** isolate, measure, prioritize and change incrementally.
