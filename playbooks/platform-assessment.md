# Playbook: Platform Assessment

## Assessment layers
1. Business capability.
2. User experience.
3. Application architecture.
4. Data architecture.
5. Integration architecture.
6. Infrastructure/cloud.
7. Security.
8. DevOps.
9. Operations.
10. Team and knowledge model.

## Output
- Platform health score.
- Risk matrix.
- Modernization options.
- Cost/value view.
