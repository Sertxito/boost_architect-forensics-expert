# Playbook: Takeover Project

## Scenario
You arrive at a client and inherit a platform with pressure, low documentation and operational risk.

## First moves
1. Do not propose a rewrite.
2. Stabilize knowledge and access.
3. Identify business-critical flows.
4. Identify release process and rollback ability.
5. Find top operational risks.
6. Build dependency and ownership map.
7. Create 30/60/90-day plan.

## 30/60/90 Plan
- 30 days: understand, stabilize, monitor, document critical flows.
- 60 days: reduce top risks and automate fragile processes.
- 90 days: define modernization roadmap and target architecture.
