# Playbook: Technical Due Diligence

## Goal
Determine whether a platform is safe to buy, migrate, outsource or invest in.

## Assessment dimensions
- Business criticality.
- Code and architecture quality.
- Security exposure.
- Cloud/infrastructure maturity.
- Team dependency.
- Vendor lock-in.
- Obsolescence.
- Operational risk.
- Modernization cost.

## Final decision
KEEP / REFACTOR / MODERNIZE / REPLATFORM / REBUILD.
